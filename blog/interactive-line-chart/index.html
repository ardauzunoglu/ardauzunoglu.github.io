Figures often carry an argument more directly than prose. This small template treats a plot as a first-class part of a post: it can unfold over time, retain a compact caption, and still expose the values behind every mark.

Other scientific figure types

The same figure shell also supports relationships, estimates with uncertainty, and matrix-valued results.

What this template is for

Future posts can swap in real data and labels while keeping the slow reveal and point-level inspection. The surrounding structure is intentionally neutral enough for a technical note, an unfinished idea, or something entirely unrelated to research.
